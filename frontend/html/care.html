Care Tips

Keep your plants, vegetables, tubers and spices thriving

01 · Ornamentals

Plant Care Basics

Caladiums, heliconias and begonias reward a little routine. Get light, water and warmth right and the colour takes care of itself.

Light

Most caladiums and begonias love bright, indirect light. Heliconias prefer sun to partial shade.

Watering

Keep caladiums and heliconias evenly moist. Let begonias dry slightly at the surface between waterings.

Warmth & Humidity

Tropical plants enjoy warm rooms and good humidity. Avoid cold drafts.

Soil & Feeding

Use a light, well-draining mix and feed lightly during the growing season.

02 · Kitchen garden

Vegetable & Tuber Care

From grow-bags to open beds, give your harvest sun, loose soil and a well-timed start with the rains.

Sun

Most vegetables and tubers need 6+ hours of direct sun for a healthy harvest.

Planting Tubers

Plant tubers or corm pieces in loose, well-drained soil or deep grow-bags, and earth up as shoots grow.

Seeds & Seedlings

Sow gourds, okra and cowpea at the start of the rains. Give climbers a trellis.

Harvest

Most tubers are ready once leaves yellow. Pick okra and beans young for the best flavour.

03 · Spices

Spice Plant & Whole Spice Care

Grow pepper, cardamom, clove, nutmeg, cinnamon and vanilla at home, then dry and store your whole spices so they stay fragrant for months.

Shade & Humidity

Cardamom, nutmeg, clove and vanilla enjoy dappled shade and humid air. Pepper vines like morning sun with afternoon shade.

Watering

Keep spice saplings evenly moist but never waterlogged. Mulch the base to hold moisture through the dry months.

Support & Soil

Train pepper and vanilla vines up a pole or tree. Use rich, humus-heavy, well-draining soil and top up with compost.

Harvest

Pick pepper berries as the first turn red, cardamom pods when plump and green, and clove buds before they open.

Drying

Dry spices in light shade or gentle sun on a clean mat until they snap. Turmeric fingers need to be boiled, then dried.

Storing Whole Spices

Keep them whole in airtight glass jars, away from heat, light and damp. Grind only what you need for the best aroma.
