Our Story

From rare tropical plants to the whole kitchen garden

Golden Aura began with a simple love of rare tropical plants — caladiums, heliconias and begonias that bring colour into every home.

Today we also grow vegetables and tubers for the kitchen garden, and spice plants and whole spices from the Kerala hills: elephant foot yam, cassava, taro, okra, drumstick, pepper, cardamom, clove and more.

Every plant is healthy, carefully packed and delivered with care.
